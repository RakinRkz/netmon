# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

First release.

- Live speed in the status bar icon (total, download or upload; bytes or bits per second), updated
  every second, with today's mobile and Wi-Fi usage in the notification.
- Live speed screen with a 60-second chart, session totals and peak, today's usage and the current
  billing cycle.
- 30 days of daily mobile and Wi-Fi usage as a chart and a table, from Android's network statistics.
- Per-app usage for today, 7 days, 30 days or the billing cycle, split into mobile and Wi-Fi.
- Monthly and daily mobile data limits with warning and limit-reached alerts.
- Optional floating speed meter and a Quick Settings tile.
- Counts hotspot and VPN traffic once (Android 12 and later).
- Light and dark themes. No internet permission.
